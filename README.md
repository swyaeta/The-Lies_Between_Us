# The Lies Between Us

*Everyone has a story. Not everyone tells the truth.*

## About

Marta and Juli have questions. The people around them have secrets. And the more they try to make sense of what's happening, the harder it becomes to tell the truth from a lie.

**The Lies Between Us** is an original story-driven visual novel about identity, trust, and the things people hide from one another. What begins with uncertainty slowly unfolds into something far more complicated, where assumptions can be dangerous and the truth isn't always what it seems.

Because sometimes, the biggest lies come from the people we least expect.

## The Story

Follow Marta and Juli as they piece together the strange events unfolding around them. With unanswered questions, unsettling suspicions, and people who may not be who they claim to be, every new discovery raises another question.


## About the Project

This project is being developed with Ren'Py, an open-source engine for creating visual novels. It combines character dialogue, visual storytelling, and background music to bring the story to life.
